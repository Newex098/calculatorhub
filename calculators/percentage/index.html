Percentage Formulas Explained

A percentage represents a fraction of 100 (from Latin per centum, meaning "by the hundred"). Below are the foundational mathematical formulas for standard percentage operations:

1. Percentage of a Number (What is X% of Y?)

Percentage = (X ÷ 100) × Y
Divide the percentage by 100 to obtain its decimal multiplier, then multiply by the total number.

2. Finding What Percentage X Is of Y

Percentage = (X ÷ Y) × 100
Divide the part (X) by the total (Y) and multiply by 100 to express the ratio as a percentage.

3. Percentage Increase

Increase % = ((New − Original) ÷ Original) × 100
Subtract the original value from the new value, divide by the original value, and multiply by 100.

4. Percentage Decrease

Decrease % = ((Original − New) ÷ Original) × 100
Subtract the new value from the original, divide by the original, and multiply by 100.

5. Percentage Difference

Difference % = |A − B| ÷ ((A + B) ÷ 2) × 100
Used when comparing two non-sequential values where neither is considered the initial baseline. Divide the absolute difference by the average of both values.

6. Retail Discount & Markup Formulas

Discount Amount = Original Price × (Discount% ÷ 100)
Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Selling Price = Cost Price + (Cost Price × Markup% ÷ 100)

Original Price = Final Price ÷ (1 − (Discount% ÷ 100))

Worked Practical Examples

Step-by-step solutions to common real-world percentage calculations:

1
What is 15% of 2,000?
• Step 1: 15 ÷ 100 = 0.15
• Step 2: 0.15 × 2,000 = 300
2
80 is what percentage of 400?
• Step 1: 80 ÷ 400 = 0.20
• Step 2: 0.20 × 100 = 20%
3
Price increases from ₹1,000 to ₹1,200
• Step 1: Change = ₹1,200 − ₹1,000 = ₹200
• Step 2: ₹200 ÷ ₹1,000 = 0.20
• Step 3: 0.20 × 100 = 20% increase
4
₹2,000 item with a 25% discount
• Discount: ₹2,000 × 0.25 = ₹500
• Final Price: ₹2,000 − ₹500 = ₹1,500
5
Cost ₹1,000 with a 30% markup
• Markup Profit: ₹1,000 × 0.30 = ₹300
• Selling Price: ₹1,000 + ₹300 = ₹1,300

Where Are Percentages Used in Everyday Life?

Percentages are among the most versatile mathematical tools used across personal finance, commerce, education, and health:

🛍️ Shopping & Discounts

Calculating retail sales, seasonal discounts ("30% off"), promotional coupons, and cash-back rewards on purchases.

🎓 Academic Scores & Exams

Converting total test marks into percentages or percentiles for university admissions and academic evaluations.

💼 Salary Appraisals & Raises

Evaluating annual compensation adjustments, bonus payouts, commission rates, and cost-of-living increases.

📈 Business Margins & Markup

Establishing profitable pricing for wholesale goods, calculating gross margins, and evaluating inventory turnover.

🏛️ Taxes & Statutory Duties

Computing GST slabs, customs tariffs, income tax deductions, and municipal property taxes.

📊 Statistics & Metrics

Tracking demographic changes, market share comparisons, election polling ratios, and performance metrics.
